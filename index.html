Our Story

The Art of Perfect Coffee

Men's Coffee was born from a simple belief: that every man deserves a moment of pure indulgence in his day. We've dedicated ourselves to crafting the perfect cup of coffee that transcends the ordinary.

Our journey began in the highlands of Ethiopia, where we discovered our first batch of heirloom coffee beans. That moment sparked a passion that would take us across continents, from the misty mountains of Colombia to the volcanic soils of Indonesia.

We work directly with small-scale farmers who share our vision of quality over quantity. Every bean is hand-picked at peak ripeness, processed with care, and roasted in small batches to unlock its unique flavor profile.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our coffee

How fresh is your coffee?

We roast all our coffee to order, meaning your beans are roasted within 24-48 hours of your order. Coffee is at its peak freshness 2-14 days after roasting, which is exactly when it arrives at your door.

What's the difference between light, medium, and dark roast?

Light roasts have a milder taste with more acidity and original bean flavors. Medium roasts offer balanced flavor, aroma, and acidity. Dark roasts have a bold, rich taste with lower acidity and more roasty notes.

How should I store my coffee?

Store your coffee in an airtight container away from light, heat, and moisture. Our bags have one-way valves that keep coffee fresh. For best results, consume within 3-4 weeks of opening.

Do you offer subscriptions?

Yes! Our subscription plans let you receive your favorite coffees automatically. Choose weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly delivery. Subscribers save 15% on every order and get access to exclusive beans.

What's your return policy?

We offer a 100% satisfaction guarantee. If you're not happy with your coffee, contact us within 30 days for a full refund or exchange. No questions asked.

Where do you source your beans?

We source directly from small farms and cooperatives in Ethiopia, Colombia, Brazil, Guatemala, and Indonesia. All our beans are fair trade certified and sustainably grown.
